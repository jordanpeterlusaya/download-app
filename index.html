Biashara yetu

HAUL ni jukwaa / marketplace la teknolojia nchini Tanzania — linaunganisha mahitaji (wateja wanaotaka kusafirisha) na ugavi (Pilots, madereva, na kampuni/transporters za mizigo). Hatufanyi kazi kama kampuni ya usafirishaji: hatumiliki meli ya magari wala hatuendeshi magari wenyewe. Tunatoa teknolojia ya kuagiza, matching, kufuatilia, malipo, na portals — washirika wetu ndio wanaofanya usafirishaji.

HAUL is a Tanzanian logistics marketplace / connector platform. Customers request delivery; we match them with partner Pilots and transporters. We are not a transportation company — we do not own or operate a carrier fleet. Tracking, payments, and partner portals run on our platform.

  1. 01

    HAUL — App ya wateja

    Wateja huomba usafirishaji wa parcel na cargo (TOYO, Kirikuu, Canter), hufuatilia safari, na kulipa kupitia app — HAUL inaunganisha oda yao na washirika waliothibitishwa.

  2. 02

    HAUL Pilots — App ya madereva

    Madereva / Pilots waliothibitishwa hupokea oda za karibu kupitia app, hukamilisha safari, na kulipa kamisheni kwa USSD (M-Pesa, Mixx, Airtel Money).

  3. 03

    Portal ya transporters / mawakala

    Kampuni na mawakala wa mizigo huendesha oda zao, matching, na malipo ya kamisheni kupitia portal — wao ni washirika wa usafirishaji, si meli ya HAUL.

  4. 04

    Matching, tracking & malipo

    Teknolojia yetu inaunganisha oda na Pilots/transporters, huonyesha hali ya safari kwa wateja, na husimamia kamisheni kwa malipo ya simu.
