Biashara yetu

HAUL ni kampuni ya teknolojia ya usafirishaji nchini Tanzania. Tunajenga mifumo inayounganisha oda, madereva, na kampuni za mizigo — kutoka kuagiza hadi kufuatilia na malipo.

HAUL is a Tanzanian logistics technology company. We connect customers, drivers, and cargo companies — booking, matching, live tracking, and commission payments.

  1. 01

    HAUL — App ya wateja

    Wateja huagiza parcel na cargo (TOYO, Kirikuu, Canter), hufuatilia safari, na kulipa kupitia app ya Android.

  2. 02

    HAUL Pilots — App ya madereva

    Madereva waliothibitishwa hupokea oda za karibu, hukamilisha safari, na kulipa kamisheni kwa USSD (M-Pesa, Mixx, Airtel Money).

  3. 03

    Portal ya wauzaji / transporters

    Kampuni za mizigo huendesha oda, matching, na malipo ya kamisheni kupitia portal ya transporter/agent.

  4. 04

    Matching, tracking & malipo

    Mfumo wetu unaunganisha oda na madereva/kampuni, huonyesha hali ya safari, na husimamia kamisheni kwa njia ya malipo ya simu.
